Welcome Back to Planet Zi.

A weapons experiment sends Planet Zi’s eras crashing together, drawing Van, Bit, Prince Athle and Zan into the same world. Now young pilot Zeru and his companion Juno must uncover what happened and find a way to put it right. Recruit familiar faces, build and customize your Zoids™, and battle to restore the world and bring its warriors home.

Zoids™ Legacy: RE:TRANSLATION revisits Zeru and Juno’s journey with a fresh English script drawn from the Japanese original. Familiar anime voices guide the dialogue, while clearer menus and more readable text bring the adventure back to the Game Boy Advance™ screen.

Made for the small screen

Proportional lettering and new line breaks give conversations a more natural flow. Menu labels fit their windows, and neatly aligned stats make your next equipment check easier.

Names you know

Brad and Jamie return under their anime names. Character labels and dialogue follow the same naming choices, from the first introduction to the pilot roster.

Know your next move

Check a Deck Command’s pilot requirements before committing a turn. Revised battle descriptions explain its effects, and skill names make a pilot’s strengths and weaknesses easier to spot.

A cast with character

Harry gets to be theatrical, and Brad gets his dry wit back. Dialogue draws on the cast’s anime voices, including familiar dub lines in scenes adapted from the show.
